## Timeline (adjust to your date)

| Phase | Focus | Duration (typical) |
|-------|--------|-------------------|
| 1 | Patterns + medium problems daily | 4–8 weeks |
| 2 | Hard problems + timed mocks | 3–6 weeks |
| 3 | LLD + system design + behavioral | parallel / 4–8 weeks |
| 4 | Full mocks (coding + design + stories) | 2–4 weeks |

**Daily rhythm (example):** 1–2 coding problems (one new pattern review), 30–60 min design reading or one LLD sketch, 1 behavioral story refinement weekly.

---

## Behavioral & process

- **STAR** stories: conflict, failure, leadership, ambiguity, scale/metrics.
- Know **your** projects: constraints, metrics, what you’d redo.
- **Questions for interviewer:** team topology, on-call, definition of success for role.

---

## Weekly review template

1. List topics still weak from timed sessions.
2. Redo 2 missed problems cold.
3. One LLD whiteboard from [lld.md](lld.md) checklist.
4. One system design deep dive with notes saved ([system-design.md](system-design.md)).
5. Refine one behavioral story with metrics.
