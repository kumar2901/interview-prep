## 🎯 Success Metrics

Interview is going well when:

✅ Naturally mention observability, cost, and tradeoffs
✅ Discuss failure modes before being asked
✅ Tie system decisions to business metrics
✅ Explain why (not just what) concepts matter
✅ Connect to your own experience effortlessly
✅ Ask thoughtful questions about the company
✅ Feel confident discussing unfamiliar topics
✅ Interviewer nods and takes notes (good sign!)

## 📞 Questions About Implementation

**Q: Should I worry about all 26 concepts?**
A: No. Focus on 🔴 CRITICAL (5) + 🟠 HIGH (10) first. That's 15 topics—very doable in 8 weeks.

**Q: How do I prioritize if I have limited time?**
A: Use ROLE-AND-COMPANY-GUIDE.md for your role + company. It tells you exactly which topics to focus on.

**Q: Can I skip some topics?**
A: Yes. But at a senior level, being asked about cost optimization, security, or incidents is highly likely. Skip only if you're confident they won't come up.

**Q: How much time should I spend on each topic?**
A: Read (30-60 min) + Implement (30-90 min) + Practice (20 min) = ~2-3 hours per topic.

**Q: Should I go deep on all topics or breadth?**
A: Depth on core topics (cost, ML, data, security) + breadth on others. See QUICK-REFERENCE-STUDY-MAP.md.
