## Shortcut paths by time budget

### You have ~20 minutes today

1. This index (steps **1–2** only).  
2. [overview.md](overview.md) timeline + weekly template.  
3. Open [leetcode-topics.md](leetcode-topics.md) or [system-design.md](system-design.md) depending on nearest interview round.

### You have one evening to orient (senior targeting MAANG)

1. Steps **1 → 7** skim (know what exists).  
2. [ROLE-AND-COMPANY-GUIDE.md](ROLE-AND-COMPANY-GUIDE.md) for your combo.  
3. [MISSING-SENIOR-CONCEPTS.md](MISSING-SENIOR-CONCEPTS.md) **Quick Prep Priority Matrix** only → write down 5 topics to study.

### Coding-heavy next two weeks

[overview.md](overview.md) → [leetcode-topics.md](leetcode-topics.md) daily → [concurrency-faqs.md](concurrency-faqs.md) if interviews include parallelism.

[overview.md](overview.md) → [system-design.md](system-design.md) + [core-concepts.md](core-concepts.md) interleaved → [lld.md](lld.md) sketches → senior extras from step **9** only where relevant.
